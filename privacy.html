Privacy Policy

Humptulips River Retreat Wildlife · Last updated October 4, 2026

Humptulips River Retreat Wildlife ("the app") is a private tool used by the owner of the Humptulips River Retreat to upload wildlife videos from the property's own cameras to the property's own YouTube, Instagram and TikTok accounts. It has no other users.

What the app accesses

With the owner's permission, through each platform's official login, the app can:

  • upload videos to the property's accounts, and
  • read basic account information (the channel or account name), only to confirm a video is going to the right account.

It does not read, collect or store anything about viewers, followers or any other person, and it does not read other content on the accounts.

How information is stored

The login tokens the platforms issue are stored only on the owner's private server, and are used only to upload the property's videos. They are not shared with, sold to or transferred to anyone.

Google user data

The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. The app uses the YouTube API Services; see the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time: for Google and YouTube at myaccount.google.com/permissions, for Instagram under Settings > Website permissions > Apps and websites, and for TikTok under Settings > Security > Apps and services. Revoking access deletes nothing from the accounts; the app simply can no longer upload.
